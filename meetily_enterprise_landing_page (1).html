Questions enterprise teams ask

Clear answers before you evaluate.

Does meeting audio leave the device?

On Meetily's default local transcription path, audio capture and transcription run on the device and audio does not leave that machine. Data flow can vary by configuration, so review the selected transcription and summary providers before deployment.

Can we choose how AI summaries are generated?

Meetily supports local AI for summaries and configurable providers using your own API key. If you choose an external provider, transcript text may be sent to that provider for summarization; audio remains local according to the product's stated workflow.

Is Meetily open source?

Meetily's Community Edition is open source under the MIT license. Review the current repository and edition details to understand what is included and what may require a paid plan.

Does using Meetily automatically make us compliant?

No software alone guarantees regulatory compliance. Local-first processing can support a privacy and data-governance strategy, but your organization still needs to assess its configuration, policies, access controls, retention and applicable legal obligations.

How do we discuss enterprise requirements?

Visit Meetily's official website to contact the team and discuss your environment, deployment requirements and the capabilities available for your organization.
